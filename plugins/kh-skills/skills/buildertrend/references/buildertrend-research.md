# Buildertrend — research report (compiled 2026-07)

What Buildertrend is, what it does, what it costs, and where it's weak — the
background for the KH `buildertrend` skill. Facts compiled from buildertrend.com
feature pages, help center, press coverage, and review sites (Capterra ~4.5/5,
G2 ~4.2/5). Pricing changes often; re-verify before quoting externally.

## The product in one paragraph

Buildertrend (buildertrend.com) is the market-leading cloud construction
management platform for **residential** builders — custom home builders,
remodelers, and specialty contractors, typically 1–100 employees. Founded 2006
in Omaha, NE by Dan Houghton and Steve & Jeff Dugger; ~1M users in ~100
countries; celebrated its 20th anniversary in early 2026. It sells an
all-in-one: sales/CRM, project management, financials, client communication,
and materials — web + iOS/Android. Everything hangs off one central object,
the **Job**, organized under six menus: Sales, Project Management, Files,
Messaging, Financial, Reports/Settings.

## Core terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Job** | The project container — every record lives under a Job |
| **Job Template** | Reusable blueprint: pre-built schedule, selection sheets, to-do checklists, folders, budget structure |
| **Cost Codes / Cost Catalog** | Construction chart-of-accounts + reusable priced line items; maps to QuickBooks/Xero |
| **Owner Invoice** | Invoice from builder to homeowner (draw / progress bill) |
| **Allowance** | Budgeted dollar amount for an undecided item, drawn down by Selections |
| **User types** | Internal Users (paid staff), Subs/Vendors (free, unlimited), Clients/Owners (free, per job, see only what's shared) |

## Modules

**Leads / CRM** — Lead Opportunities with stages (Qualified → Estimating →
Proposal Sent → Sold/Lost), confidence %, source, salesperson; Lead Activities
(calls/meetings with reminders); email drip marketing; website contact-form
capture; Lead Proposals with e-signature that convert to Jobs on acceptance.

**Scheduling** — Schedule Items with duration, assignees (staff + subs), phase
color coding, and **predecessor links with lag**; slipping one item cascades
downstream automatically. Gantt / calendar / list views; baseline vs. actual;
schedule templates applied per job. Subs get auto email/text/push when items
are created or shifted and **confirm/decline** assignments.

**Daily Logs** — dated jobsite diary: notes, photos/videos, tags,
**auto-populated weather** (delay-claim evidence). Per-log share toggles for
subs/owner; supers post from mobile at end of day.

**To-Do's** — tasks with assignees, priority, due dates (fixed or tied to a
schedule item, e.g. "3 days before Drywall"), checklists, photos. The punch
list is photo-annotated to-dos assigned to responsible subs.

**Change Orders** — scope/price changes with line-item pricing, builder cost
vs. owner price, attachments. Workflow: draft → released to owner → owner
approves/declines **online with e-signature** (payment can be required at
approval) → approved amount flows into budget and owner invoicing. Statuses:
Pending / Approved / Declined / Expired. Core promise: nothing extra gets
built without a signed, priced record.

**Selections & Allowances** — client decision points with Choices (photos,
specs, vendor, price), organized by category/location, with **deadlines linked
to schedule items** ("choose tile 14 days before Tile Install"). Client picks
draw against allowances; overage/underage computed automatically and rolls to
budget/invoices. E-signature approval; approvals can auto-release linked POs.
Reviewers call this the single biggest client-management win in custom building.

**Estimates, Bids & Proposals** — line-item estimates by cost code from the
Cost Catalog with markup controls (fixed-price or cost-plus); client-facing
Proposals (lump-sum or line detail) with online approval and deposit
collection; Bid Packages sent to multiple subs, side-by-side comparison, award
converts to a PO and into the budget.

**Budget / Job Costing** — per cost code: original budget → approved COs →
**committed** (open POs/bills) → **actual** (paid bills, time-clock labor) →
variance and projected margin. Two-way QuickBooks (Online/Desktop) and Xero
sync.

**Purchase Orders & Bills** — POs commit cost to a sub/vendor; vendors accept
electronically; bills record against POs; online sub payment with lien-waiver
collection. POs feed "committed cost" for forward-looking control.

**Owner Invoices & Buildertrend Payments** — milestone draws / progress
bills; homeowners pay **online via ACH or card** from the portal; builders pay
subs electronically. Major monetization push since ~2021.

**Client Portal** — homeowner login + app showing only what the builder
exposes: schedule milestones, shared daily logs and photos, selections
awaiting decision, COs awaiting signature, invoices/payments, documents,
warranty, messaging. The sales differentiator ("watch your home from your
phone") and system of record for every client signature.

**Sub Portal** — free unlimited sub accounts: confirm schedule, receive bid
requests, accept POs, upload COIs with expiration tracking, get paid online.

**Messaging / Comments / Surveys** — threaded comments on almost any record
with per-audience visibility; per-job email inbox; configurable notifications;
milestone surveys (e.g. post-closing satisfaction); RFIs for formal Q&A.

**Documents / Photos / Videos** — per-job folder trees, per-file sharing,
mobile upload, PDF/plan markup and annotation.

**Time Clock** — mobile clock in/out tagged to job + cost code, geofencing,
shift map, overtime rules; flows to job costing as actual labor; payroll
export (QuickBooks, Gusto).

**Warranty** — homeowner submits claims with photos via portal; builder
schedules service appointments, assigns the responsible sub, closes with owner
sign-off. Keeps 11-month-walkthrough season organized.

**Takeoff & Materials** — digital takeoff from plans feeding estimate line
items; materials purchasing/rebate programs; CBUSA group-purchasing arm.

**Reports / Dashboards** — lead conversion by source, schedule performance,
budget vs. actual, WIP report, job profitability, open POs, unpaid invoices;
built-in Buildertrend Analytics dashboards plus a paid "Business Insights"
custom-reporting service.

**Recent product direction (2025–26)** — "Tasks" is replacing To-Do's (new
architecture, inline editing, dedicated Punchlists tab); **AI Client Updates**
(June 2025) auto-drafts weekly client summaries from daily logs and the
schedule (claimed 97% time savings); Cost Inbox OCR receipt capture; AI Bill
Pay (IBS, Feb 2026); refreshed Client Portal; Draw Schedules on fixed-price
jobs; multi-signature CO approvals.

## The lead → warranty workflow

1. **Lead capture** (website form → Lead Opportunity → activities/pipeline).
2. **Estimate & proposal** (takeoff → estimate by cost code → e-signed proposal, deposit collected).
3. **Conversion** — lead becomes a Job; Job Template instantiates schedule, selections, checklists, folders, budget; owner gets portal login; draw schedule set.
4. **Pre-construction** — bid packages to subs; awarded bids → POs; selections released with deadlines sequenced ahead of the schedule.
5. **Construction** — run the schedule (shifts cascade + auto-notify), daily logs with photos/weather, to-dos, time clock; scope changes become signed COs; overages roll to budget; owner invoices at draw milestones, paid online; QuickBooks sync.
6. **Closeout** — photo punch list assigned to subs, final invoice, lien waivers, closing survey.
7. **Warranty** — portal claims → service appointments → sign-off.

## Pricing (verify before quoting — changed in 2026)

- Last published tiers (through 2025): **Essential** $499/mo monthly (~$339/mo
  billed annually) — scheduling, logs, to-dos, client portal, basic CRM and
  invoicing, but **no estimating, change orders, bids, or POs**; **Advanced**
  $799/mo (~$499/mo annual) — adds the full financial suite: estimating,
  takeoff, change orders, bid requests, POs, budget; **Complete** $1,099/mo
  (~$829/mo annual) — adds the client selections portal, warranty management,
  RFIs, and advanced reporting. First-month promos of $199/$499/$799 were
  routinely misquoted as regular prices. Flat per-company, **unlimited users
  and projects** on every tier.
- **2026: public pricing removed** — now volume-based, quote-only via a
  five-step sales form with 11 annual-construction-volume brackets ($0–499K up
  to $31M+); price scales with the builder's revenue, and high-volume quotes
  reportedly exceed $50k/yr.
- Terms: new customers typically sign an **annual contract**; auto-renewal
  with a built-in CPI increase of **up to 10%** per Buildertrend's T&C;
  ~30-day cancellation notice, no mid-contract refund. Onboarding runs
  $400–2,000 for complex setups ("Buildertrend Boost" coaching included with
  annual contracts). Buildertrend Payments fees: cards 2.99% + $0.30 + $1.49
  platform fee (Amex 3.95%); ACH 0.5%, $1.99 min, $15 max per transfer.
  Real-world spend commonly ~$8,000–10,000/yr. Annual billing saves ~$2–3k/yr.
- Recurring complaints: renewal price hikes, hard cancellation, no bulk data
  export when leaving.

## API & integrations

- **No open public API, no webhooks, no Zapier/Make/Workato app.** A REST API
  exists at api.buildertrend.com but it is partner-gated — no developer
  portal, no self-serve keys, no published spec. Programmatic access for a
  regular customer means (a) the curated Marketplace integrations, (b)
  per-grid Excel/CSV exports ("Export" button on any grid that supports it),
  or (c) unofficial reverse-engineered tools (Supergood's unofficial
  API/MCP server, community browser-automation bots). This is exactly why a
  file-based KH system Claude can read/write is attractive.
- Native integrations: QuickBooks Online/Desktop (flagship 2-way sync) and
  Xero, Gusto payroll (hourly employees + time clock), HubSpot / Salesforce /
  Pipedrive lead sync, The Home Depot Pro Xtra (cost catalog + PO purchasing,
  daily price refresh), GreenSky client financing, schedule sync to
  Google Calendar / Outlook / iCal, and built-in Buildertrend Payments
  (runs on WePay/Chase — ACH, cards, Apple/Google Pay, up to $500K/txn).

## Company history

2006 founded (Omaha) by Dan Houghton and brothers Jeff & Steve Dugger,
bootstrapped ~14 years. **Dec 2020**: first outside investment, led by Bain
Capital Tech Opportunities with HGGC (amount undisclosed). **Feb 2021:
acquired CoConstruct**, its largest rival — ~23,000 combined clients, 1.1M+
users, $200B+ annual project value. CoConstruct got no updates after 2021 and
is being retired (new projects must run in Buildertrend; historical data stays
read-only) — the forced migration is a lasting churn event that JobTread
actively targets. CBUSA (the largest US homebuilder group-purchasing org) came
via CoConstruct, which had bought it in Oct 2020. Acquired **Square Takeoff**
(launched as Buildertrend Takeoff, unveiled 2022–23). **Jan 2023: full
rebrand** ("Work Simpler") plus the payments/financing/insurance fintech push.
**Nov 2023: laid off 16% of staff** (~800 remained; ~700+ and 20,000+
customers as of 2026), which reviewers link to a support-quality dip. Dan
Houghton is still CEO through 2026; Jon Walker CTO (2023), Bob Drake CFO
(2023), Brooke Brockman CMO (2024), Charlotte Bradley CPO (May 2025).

## Competitors

| Competitor | vs. Buildertrend |
|---|---|
| **JobTread** ($159/mo first user + $18/user, annual; all features every plan) | #1 grassroots alternative — G2 ~4.9 vs BT's ~4.2; open API + Zapier; stronger estimating/job costing; CEO answers questions on Reddit. Weaker scheduling (no dependencies/baselines) and thinner selections/portal. The main defection path, with a dedicated CoConstruct-migration program. |
| **Procore** (quote, ~0.1–0.2% of construction volume; $15k–80k+/yr) | Enterprise/commercial GC focus (RFIs, submittals); overkill under ~$10M revenue. |
| **Houzz Pro** (~$85–399/mo, also moving to volume tiers) | Design-forward: 3D floor plans, mood boards, Houzz lead gen; scheduling lacks dependencies/Gantt; notorious auto-renewal billing complaints. |
| **BuildBook** ($79–299/mo + $20/user) | Simple and cheap ("everything on one page"); popular downgrade, but outgrown fast — no selections/warranty depth. |
| **Contractor Foreman** ($49–332/mo) | ~10x cheaper at entry, offline-capable mobile; dated UI, shallow estimating. |
| **CompanyCam** (~$79–249/mo plans) | Complement, not competitor — best-of-breed photo documentation; integrates with Buildertrend. |

Segmentation consensus: Procore (commercial/enterprise) > Buildertrend
(established residential, $1M+ volume — BT itself says it's not suited under
~$500K revenue) > JobTread (small–mid residential, estimating-first) > Houzz
Pro (design-led remodelers) > BuildBook / Contractor Foreman (small crews).

## Sentiment summary

Ratings: Capterra 4.5/5 (~2,500 reviews), GetApp 4.6, Software Advice 4.5,
G2 ~4.2, TrustRadius 7.4/10, Trustpilot low. The high-volume legacy pools skew
positive; newer/smaller pools skew down — consistent with declining sentiment
after the CoConstruct acquisition and Nov 2023 layoffs.

**Praised**: sub scheduling notifications, client portal transparency
("clients love seeing progress in real time"), selections/allowance
discipline, CO e-signatures, all-in-one consolidation, unlimited users and
storage. **Criticized**:

- **Price hikes** — the #1 complaint: renewal increases of 50–65% reported,
  "$350/mo → $800/mo," "doubled the cost from one month to the next — they
  know you have too much time invested to switch."
- **Lock-in** — annual contracts strictly enforced, no early-termination
  refund, and **no bulk export** of years of files/photos/records on the way
  out.
- **Estimating** called complicated and time-consuming vs. dedicated tools.
- **UI/learning curve** — "10x more clicking than there needs to be";
  templates and cost codes take weeks to set up; modules feel siloed ("a mile
  wide, an inch thick").
- **Reporting limits** ("how much have I invoiced this month across all
  projects?" not possible without Excel export), QuickBooks sync quirks,
  mobile-app glitches, and post-CoConstruct support decline ("skeleton crew,
  East Coast hours").

Best fit per reviewers: residential builders/remodelers wanting one system for
scheduling + client portal + daily logs who can absorb ~$500–1,100/mo and
weeks of onboarding.
